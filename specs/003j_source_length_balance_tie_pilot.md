## Separate coverage, tie rule and additional search

1. Fresh v4.1-style discovery includes accepted v3/v4/v4.1 selected sets for all exploratory budgets as incumbents. After discovery re-evaluate and retain all accepted v4 ordinary evaluations and v4.1 shared candidates from complete originals; exclude feasibility-only singleton rescue. Reusing candidate IDs is declared; previous cached scores/tokens are not trusted. Deduplicate chronological IDs, exact feasibility/Pareto/unchanged anchor/count/hash choice gives the **primary control**. Do not claim a from-scratch search or global optimum.
2. For each business, compute BODY token lengths without structural markers/special tokens for ALL its original English reviews. Sort lengths; cutpoints are order statistics at indices floor((n-1)*q/4), q=1,2,3. Each review is assigned with bisect_right(cutpoints,length) to four bins. Equal lengths stay together; duplicate cutpoints/empty bins are allowed and source proportions use actual counts (not assumed25% each). All lengths identical is valid. Binning does not filter reviews. Four bins is a prespecified pilot parameter, not a final frozen research choice.
3. Form the group in the feasible primary frontier whose review COUNT, five STAR counts and five TIME-bin counts exactly match the primary control. This stronger equality preserves primary JSD/weighted cost/count/comparability. Choose minimum base2 JSD between source and selected BODY-length-bin histograms. Remaining ties use the existing seed-derived hash/chronological IDs, never shortest/longest/fullest block or length mean. This is the **existing-candidates secondary rule**.
4. Starting from that choice, run at most six secondary refinement iterations. For each outgoing review, offer at most one seeded incoming original per source length bin with the same star and source time bin; exclude selected originals. Rank proposals by length JSD/hash, encode the top four exactly, retain only complete blocks fitting the configured budget. Accept only a STRICT length-JSD reduction (numerical1e-12); no neutral hash wandering. Every replacement preserves the joint star/time cell, count and primary marginals. Retain all feasible evaluated alternatives; choose length-JSD/hash among the original equivalent group and these candidates. This is the **bounded-refinement secondary rule**. Record evaluated/unevaluated proposal counts, operations and exact pre/post tokens/histograms/JSD. No requirement to fill tokens or to reduce short20 to zero; shortness is a diagnostic, not a quality label.
5. Keep primary control fixed while refining; do not feed secondary proposals back into the primary quality anchor. The ablations separate tie-only gains from additional search; their available secondary candidates differ by design. No cross-budget forced count monotonicity; report any drops. Final chosen states fit final budget, all accepted secondary intermediate states fit their budget. Export primary control IDs and whole-review replacement differences.
