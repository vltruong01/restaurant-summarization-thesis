Scope and reproducibility

Methodology

A presentation of existing experimental evidence, with no new inference or human evaluation.

Experimental design

  • 15 external TripAdvisor restaurants; no additional LED fine-tuning on these restaurants.
  • 247 selected reviews and permitted restaurant metadata supply Review Consensus.
  • LED F-S1000 is task-fine-tuned, source-separated LED-large. Qwen is an external pretrained instruction-following baseline. This comparison does not isolate architecture or parameter count.
  • LED F and Qwen-Caption use the same filtered BLIP-2 captions for Visual Evidence.
  • Qwen-Image uses 73 selected image files directly for Visual Evidence. Their selection inherits Stage O caption filtering.
  • Qwen-Image reuses Qwen-Caption’s Review Consensus verbatim for all 15 restaurants.
  • Two evaluator accounts, A01 and A02, produced 90 official V1 records, containing 540 criterion scores. Later revisions and Fast Mode researcher judgments are excluded.

Reading the results

All restaurants, including poor LED cases, remain in the cohort. A restaurant mean averages the two original ratings. Overall means pool 30 ratings per system; paired higher/equal/lower counts compare 15 restaurant means. No new significance test, causal effect or composite ranking is introduced.

Qwen has higher observed means on all five non-visual criteria. LED and Qwen-Caption have equal Visual means at every restaurant. Qwen-Image has the highest aggregate Visual score, while individual cases differ. Equal scores are not evidence that the texts are equally accurate. Aggregate ratings cannot certify every statement.

Generated passages are research artifacts, not verified restaurant descriptions or recommendations. Original errors, omissions, spelling and repetition are retained. BLIP-2 captions are model-generated evidence inputs, not independent factual references.

Source integrity

The rating file digest identifies the designated original V1 collection. The original parent archive digest is historical provenance; that parent ZIP and model weights were not rehashed for this viewer. Available source exports, native generation bindings and selected JPEGs were checked directly.

Publication and privacy

Evaluator identities, credentials, notes, submission timestamps and access metadata are not included. Dates shown for source reviews are source review dates, not evaluator activity. The full local viewer must not be copied into a public repository.
