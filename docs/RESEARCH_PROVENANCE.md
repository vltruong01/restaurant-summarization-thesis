# Research provenance and interpretation

The public repository presents completed implementation, frozen experimental settings, generated outputs, numerical results and reproducible analysis. Conversation transcripts, task handoffs, application instructions and coordination diaries are not review materials.

## Assistance and recorded human decisions

Teacher targets are model-generated supervision. Stage F calibration uses a model-assisted workflow: initial suggestions may be compared with source material and explicitly confirmed or edited by the user. An optional second model opinion is an aid, not independent human ground truth. Recorded user confirmations do not independently authenticate actual image inspection. This calibration workflow is distinct from the final two-evaluator studies.

TEST100 references have disclosed model feedback, evidence suggestions and editorial assistance. Recorded author/checker approval binds the approved reference version, but does not establish unaided authorship. Evaluator account records likewise do not independently verify participant identity, independence or source inspection. Known measurement limitations and attributed participant flags remain in the notebook and final reports.

## What is preserved

Frozen scores, source/output identifiers, model/checkpoint hashes, inference policies, statistical plans, numerical verification and limitations are retained. Official Stage P analysis uses the original V1 collection; later exploratory rating revisions are excluded. No original ratings, references, outputs or conclusions have been changed during public documentation curation.

Teacher/inference prompts are experimental implementation, not conversation history. Historical metadata inside original scientific evidence is retained for provenance; it is not a task instruction or a transcript. The complete original private archive remains available separately to the owner. Removing files from the current public tree does not erase previous public Git commits.
