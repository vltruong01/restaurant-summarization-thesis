## B: resource and methodology estimate, not a benchmark

Plan for the approved **5,000 source / 768 target** envelope, not just averages. The current Filtered training source mean/max is 3,809/4,213 tokens and label mean/max 317/619; validation maxima are 4,193/562. A 5,000-token input pads to **5,120** with the frozen 1,024 attention window. Full tuning must support this envelope without reducing budgets or truncating data.

For 161,844,480 parameters, FP32 weights + gradients + AdamW first/second moments alone occupy **2.41 GiB** (16 bytes/parameter), excluding activations, AMP casts, temporary buffers and allocator overhead. One FP16 encoder attention-score-shaped array at 5,120×1,025×12 is about **120 MiB per layer**; one decoder cross-attention array at 768×5,120×12 is **90 MiB per layer**; FP16 vocabulary logits add about **74 MiB**. These illustrate shape costs, not a measured peak or an exact allocation model. Backward retention and implementations matter; LoRA's observed 2.65 GiB device usage cannot establish full-tuning feasibility.

**Planning estimate:** one 24 GB GPU, physical batch 1, mixed precision and gradient checkpointing is a reasonable first candidate; allow roughly **10–20 GiB** as a broad working-memory estimate, subject to an approved real worst-length forward/backward test. A 48 GB device offers more headroom. Neither is certified here; do not plan this rescue around the local 6 GB GPU. Recommend 32 GB host RAM and 100 GB free disk. FP32 model + Adam moments are approximately 1.81 GiB per checkpoint before extras; five checkpoints for three conditions use roughly 27 GiB before environment, caches, exports and evidence.

For a hypothetical five-epoch/effective-batch-8 ceiling: 10,000 microbatches and 1,250 updates per condition, or 30,000/3,750 across RO/U/F. D-058 averaged about 1.78 seconds per training example including validation/overheads. It does not predict full tuning or another GPU. As a conservative same-local-throughput planning sensitivity, 2–4 seconds/microbatch implies **5.6–11.1 h/condition, 16.7–33.3 h for three**, plus validation/generation/checkpoint overhead; this is an unmeasured scenario, not a speed forecast. Future measured throughput must replace it before commitment.

B requires explicit methodology approval: unfreeze the complete backbone; choose a fresh full-tuning optimizer/LR and schedule rather than reuse LoRA LR 2e-4 automatically; freeze effective batch, precision, epoch ceiling, checkpoint selection and evaluation policy identically across conditions. A lower LR (for example 1e-5–3e-5) is a proposal, not an adopted setting or a guaranteed literature recipe. Preserve the original inputs, targets, review order, caption rules, empty-visual handling, tokenizer, 5,000/4,000/750 budgets and beam4/max_length=768 decoding. Preserve old checkpoints in separate provenance. No new data filtering, Teacher regeneration or decoding search is needed.
