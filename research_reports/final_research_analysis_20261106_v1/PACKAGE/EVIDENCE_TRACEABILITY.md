## Reproduction scope

The public notebook and its standard-library verifier use bundled exports. Historical verification receipts describe checks on original complete packages; their private source archives are not all distributed here. Complete training logs and original source material require access to the private research archive. E18 links the public training contract/excerpt and archive bindings, not a complete native training log.

Stages M, N, O and P remain distinct. No new cohort exclusion, rating correction, composite ranking or statistical policy is introduced. Pointwise intervals and multiplicity-adjusted tests must retain their original interpretation. Participant flags are reported judgments, not independently confirmed errors.
